Taking back control of software through fundamental research, application, and education.

The Noelo Lab is a new research group at the University of Georgia studying assisted and automated ways to make software understandable, maintainable, secure, and robust. To achieve that mission, our group shares interests across cybersecurity, software engineering, and artificial intelligence.

Mission

Improving the world through impactful research of software systems.

The lab organizes its work around three commitments.

Advance

Through fundamental research: how software systems behave, how they fail, and what can actually be proven about them.

Apply

Through partnerships with companies, and by shortening the distance between a published result and something people can use.

Educate

Through what those advances teach us, about the subject, and about how to explain topics of importance to students and to the public.
